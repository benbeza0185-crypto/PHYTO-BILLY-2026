💧 Watering

Plants need water for photosynthesis and other life processes. Avoid both underwatering and overwatering.

🌱 Soil

🐛 Pests

About PHYTO-NET

Our mission

PHYTO-NET is a plant information and smart plant-care project. It aims to combine technology, science and agriculture to help people understand and care for plants.
