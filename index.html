Global Plant Explorer

Search scientific plant records using common or scientific names. Results come from GBIF's global biodiversity catalogue. Millions of records are searchable, but not every record has a photo or a complete care profile.

Tip: enter a common name or scientific name. Search results may include synonyms and different accepted-name records.
Ready to search global plant records.
Data source: GBIF Species API. GBIF is a biodiversity data network, not a complete plant-care or disease-treatment database. Images are searched separately and may not always be verified photographs of the exact species.
