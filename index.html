About Billy & PHYTO-BILLY-2026

PHYTO-NET is part of the PHYTO-BILLY-2026 innovation project. It is a student initiative in Rwanda exploring the connection between plant knowledge, plant care and technology.

Our mission

Knowledge for plants and people.

PHYTO-NET aims to make plant information easier to explore and understand. It brings together global plant discovery, educational resources, plant-health learning and smart monitoring ideas.

Project objectives

  • Explore plant species from global databases.
  • Learn about plant health and responsible care.
  • Support agricultural education.
  • Connect plant monitoring with Arduino technology.
  • Encourage innovation and environmental awareness.
