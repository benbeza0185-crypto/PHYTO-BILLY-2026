🩺 Plant Health Centre

Learn about common plant problems.

🍂

Yellow leaves

Possible causes include watering problems, nutrient shortages, pests or natural ageing. Check the whole plant before deciding.

⚪

Powdery mildew

A fungal disease that may look like white powder on leaves. Improve airflow and seek local agricultural advice if it spreads.

💧

Root rot

Roots can be damaged when soil remains waterlogged. Use suitable drainage and avoid excessive watering.

🐛

Pests

Inspect leaves for holes, insects and unusual marks. Identify the pest before choosing a control method.

☀️

Water stress

Wilting may indicate water stress, but can have other causes. Check soil moisture before watering.

🌱

Nutrient problems

Nutrient shortages can affect leaf colour and growth. Good soil management and suitable compost can help.

🌾 Plant Nutrition

Understand what plants need to grow.

Nitrogen

Supports leafy growth and is an important part of proteins.

Phosphorus

Helps energy transfer and supports root and reproductive development.

Potassium

Helps regulate water balance and supports many plant functions.
