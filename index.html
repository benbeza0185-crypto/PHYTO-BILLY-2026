KNOWLEDGE AND LEARNING

Plant Learning Articles

Educational reading for plant science and smart agriculture.

Why plants need water

Water helps plants transport nutrients, maintain structure and carry out photosynthesis. Different plants need different amounts of water.

Plant biology

Understanding soil moisture

Soil moisture affects root health and plant growth. A moisture sensor can help estimate when a plant may need irrigation.

Smart agriculture

How photosynthesis works

Plants use light energy, water and carbon dioxide to produce sugars and release oxygen. Chlorophyll helps capture light.

Science

Responsible plant care

Observe plants regularly, water according to their needs, provide suitable light and seek reliable advice when disease is suspected.

Plant care

Why biodiversity matters

Plant diversity supports ecosystems, food systems, habitats and resilience to environmental changes.

Environment

Technology in agriculture

Sensors, automation and digital information can support farmers in making informed crop-care decisions.

A Connect button cannot automatically connect every device. Each board needs compatible hardware, firmware, a communication method and sometimes a server or gateway.

Connect by USB Serial using a supported desktop browser. Your Arduino sketch must send sensor readings in the expected text format.

THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

About PHYTO-NET

CREATOR

Billy

PHYTO-NET is a student innovation project developed under the name PHYTO-BILLY-2026. It explores how technology can support plant knowledge, observation and responsible plant care.

The project is designed as a learning journey combining science, electronics, programming and environmental awareness.

OUR MISSION

Helping plants through knowledge and technology

Our mission is to make plant information easier to explore and demonstrate how sensors and automation can support plant care.

We believe young innovators can use science and creativity to help their communities.

Project objectives

🌱 Plant education

Make botanical information easier to access.

💧 Automated irrigation

Demonstrate how sensors can help manage watering.

🔬 Scientific thinking

Encourage observation, testing and evidence-based decisions.
